The elapsed column is a capability-report run, including adaptation, snapping,
IncidenceGraph, noding, DCEL, snapshot/index creation and report facts. It is not the
synthetic 300k scaling gate.

All 567,704 Arrangement edges have source support. Their 675,673 support fragments
retain occurrence IDs, definition IDs, direction, and normalized source-parameter
ranges even where multiple authored entities overlap one derived edge.

The 384 MLight gate compares the authored ModelSpace surface. Shared top-level entity
counts match. Its eight raw differences are all explained arithmetically:

- two DXF `POLYLINE` rows equal two MLight `2d_polyline` rows;
- 394 DXF `ATTRIB` records are nested in MLight's `block_reference.attributes`;
- 77 opaque `ACAD_PROXY_ENTITY` DXF records were not emitted by the MLight iterator;
- the four layer deltas sum to exactly those 471 representation-only records.

`matches=true` therefore means “identical or explicitly reconciled,” not that the two
representations have equal raw totals. See `summary.json` for the compact comparison and
the per-drawing JSON files for bounded details and validation diagnostics.
